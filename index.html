What is MusicMi?
MusicMi is a Music conservatory program located in Miami, providing music lessons to kids and adults.
For whom?
This program started with the intention to provide financial assistance to qualifying families for music education. Since its inception, we have successfully assisted over 500 students.
Why choose MusicMi?
MusicMi was conceptualized to provide every human being with access to a proper music education.
Join 500+ Students
At MusicMi we believe that you deserve to receive the best music education possible without needing to take any loans. Due to the accumulative fees that incur with taking private music lessons, we have realized there is a true need to provide some assistance to families in need.

OUR MISSION

At MusicMi we set a mission for ourselves to provide aspiring young musicians from across the country with an opportunity to study with world-class artists and to provide our students with a music education of the highest artistic standards, regardless of their financial situation.

THE PROGRAM

We have Partnered with exceptionally talented music teachers to provide the best possible music program available. Through this program you will receive:

  • Private One-on-One music lessons
  • Group music theory classes ( Ooptional )
  • Seasonal recitals
  • Interactive community of young musicians
  • Certificate program
